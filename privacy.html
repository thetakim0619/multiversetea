Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes how Multiverse Tea Stats (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Multiverse Tea YouTube channel, handles data. The App is used only by the channel owner to read statistics about the owner's own channel.

What the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the App uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API with read-only scopes (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read:

  • the list of videos uploaded to the owner's channel and their public details (title, publish time, view, like and comment counts);
  • aggregated analytics for those videos (views, watch time, audience retention, shares, subscribers gained);
  • public comments posted on those videos.

The App cannot upload, edit or delete anything, and it does not access any other Google account data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to measure how our own videos perform and to improve future videos. Analytics are aggregated and do not identify individual viewers. Comment text is read only to understand audience reactions.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored locally on the channel owner's computer. The OAuth token is stored only on that computer. We do not sell, share or transfer the data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Locally stored data can be deleted at any time by the owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Multiverse Tea YouTube channel.
